C. ARCHETYPES -- do compositions differ in TEXTURE?
==============================================================================
  12-card shared deck, dealt 5 and 6. 400 games each.
  Win rate should stay near even for all of them -- a shared
  deck is symmetric. The question is whether the GAME differs.

  archetype         P1   caps  big turn  margin 1   depth  play decides
  rotators       60.8%   19.5         4     76.8%   70.0%         33.0%
  chainers       47.5%   18.8         5     66.0%   70.2%         43.5%
  deniers        55.2%   19.1         4     70.0%   75.5%         36.0%
  reach          52.2%   19.3         4     63.7%   58.5%         32.5%
  sweepers       62.0%   19.6         4     78.0%   64.8%         34.5%
  priced mix     53.2%   19.1         5     70.5%   70.8%         44.0%
  everything     55.5%   19.4         5     73.2%   67.0%         33.5%

  Spread in the caps / big turn / margin columns is what makes
  an archetype real. Spread in the P1 column would instead mean
  the shared deck is not actually symmetric.
