D. TANTALUS ENCODING HAZARD
==========================================================================================

   0/0/0/0. With element 0 the packed card is 0, which engine.py
   uses as the EMPTY-CELL sentinel. The card would vanish.
   make_card(1,1,1,1, no element) packs to 4369 -> distinct
   faces 1/1/1/1 count in the legal pool: 1
   => Tantalus needs an explicit floor of 1 per face.
