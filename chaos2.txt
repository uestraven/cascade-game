  'play' + 'anti' is how often skill mattered at all; the rest

C. DO DIFFERENT ABILITIES COMBINE?
============================================================================
  holder win rate, seats swapped, 900 games

  pool                     5 copies of one   5 different
  all 13                             58.1%         57.2%
  the priced 5                       54.4%         55.3%
  board-volatile                     60.4%         60.6%

  A gap means abilities interact -- which is what a deck of 28
  individual ability cards would produce, and what measuring
  one ability at a time could never show.
